Acrylic Painting

Acrylic paint is a fast-drying synthetic medium made of pigments suspended in an acrylic polymer emulsion. For Abstract Expressionists like Jose Joya, acrylic offered the perfect balance of quick drying times, strong color intensity, and structural flexibility. Unlike oil, thick applications of acrylic dry without cracking easily, allowing artists to build heavy, sculptural impasto layers, scrape into surfaces, and mix paper collages directly onto the canvas.
