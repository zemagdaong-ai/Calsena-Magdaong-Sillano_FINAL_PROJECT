Our Founding

Siningmula combines the Filipino words "sining" (arts) and "simula" (beginning) to represent a space where artist can learn traditional mediums learned by Filipino Masters.

Our Goals

Inspire

Give new ideas by showing how Filipino Masters bring oiul, watercolor, and acrylic to life.

Encourage

Gives artists the confidence to pick up a brush and try different traditional mediums themselves.

Influence

Pass down Filipino artistry to a new generation of younger artists.

Educate

Make it easy for anyone to explore the stories, styles, and craft behind classic Filipino paintings.
