Oil Painting

Oil paint is made of fine color pigments mixed with a drying oil, usually linseed oil. Its biggest advantage is how slowly it dries, giving artists plenty of time to blend colors seamlessly, get skin tones just right, and work on light and shadow over multiple sessions. It also lets artists layer thin translucent coats called glazes to build up luminous depth, while producing rich colors that stay vibrant for centuries.
