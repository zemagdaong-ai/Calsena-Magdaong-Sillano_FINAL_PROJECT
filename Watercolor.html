Watercolor Paitning

Watercolor paint consists of fine pigments mixed with a water soluble binder, usually gum Arabic, and is applied to paper using water as its solvent. Its main feature is transparency, which lets the white of the paper shine through to create glowing highlights. Because watercolor dries fast and reacts immediately to wet washes, it requires strong brush control and quick execution since mistakes are hard to cover up or erase.
